What good writing looks like

The essential (micro) copy rules I used at Google.
Rita Kind-Envy
6 min read  ·  Jan 5, 2026
👋 4.7K 💬 290 🔖 ▶ ↑ ⋯

Writing well isn't about big words or clever phrasing — it's about clarity. At Google, the best copy always did one thing: it got out of the user's way.

Here are the micro copy rules that made the biggest difference in day-to-day product writing, from button labels to error messages.

Rule one: say less. Every extra word is a small tax on the reader's attention. Rule two: be specific. "Something went wrong" tells the user nothing; "We couldn't save your changes — check your connection" tells them what to do next.
